# AI

**At Urban Tech Creative, we leverage AI as a catalyst for co-creation, enhancing rather than replacing human narratives.**

In [Window to the Soul](/work/window-to-the-soul), the refugee testimonies we worked with were already anonymised. AI was then used to synthesise these disparate experiences into a new, cohesive narrative framework. This allowed us to responsibly combine multiple real-world accounts into structured storylines without re-identifying individuals. We also used AI to generate character profiles based on these blended narratives, enabling stakeholders to review and refine the direction of the stories before they were translated into immersive content. This created a clear and ethical approval process, balancing authenticity with sensitivity.

Similarly, in [Dock Stories](/work/dock-stories), AI supported the creation of full-body digital mannequins for each character, enabling historically grounded figures to be realised efficiently within the production pipeline. AI also assisted in developing distinct character voices, helping to shape tone, language, and personality in a way that aligned with the heritage context while remaining engaging for audiences.

In [Trailmakers](/work/trailmakers), AI is used as an educational tool, supporting students and teachers to research historical figures and co-create new digital interpretations. Through guided prompts, image generation, and storytelling workflows, participants actively shape how heritage is represented, turning passive learning into a creative, participatory process.

Across our placemaking and immersive storytelling work, AI supports adaptive AR content and personalised experiences — ensuring authenticity and collaboration remain central to every project.
